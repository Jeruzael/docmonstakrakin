1. Summary of Hardening Implemented
The trusted self-bootstrap mechanism has been hardened according to the full Step 2A specification:
WorkItem Governance Semantics (Sections 3 & 10):
WorkItems during bootstrap strictly forbid VERIFIED, APPROVED, or RELEASED.
Permitted bootstrap statuses: PROPOSED, BACKLOG, READY, IN_PROGRESS, VERIFICATION, DEFERRED.
Prior completed technical work is represented as status = 'VERIFICATION' accompanied by legitimate Evidence records in manifest.evidence.
Enforced the core invariant: Technical evidence exists ≠ authenticated human verification occurred.
Plain-text heuristic matching on acceptanceCriteria (e.g., EVID-, rc-regression) has been completely removed. Evidence linkage requires explicit foreign keys in workItem.evidence.
Governance-Bearing Artifact Protections (Section 4):
Requirements cannot be APPROVED during bootstrap (permitted: PROPOSED, UNDER_REVIEW, REJECTED, DEFERRED, VERIFIED).
ADRs cannot be ACCEPTED during bootstrap; formal ratification requires human sign-off (permitted: PROPOSED, REJECTED, DEPRECATED, SUPERSEDED).
Features cannot be APPROVED during bootstrap (permitted: PROPOSED, UNDER_REVIEW, DEFERRED, REJECTED).
No synthetic approval records are generated to back-fill or simulate historical sign-offs.
